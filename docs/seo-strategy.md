# SEO strategy — Wholesale Rakhi (RB.us)

## Role of the site

RB.us is the **authority / information / wholesale lead** property. Consumer shopping belongs on future portals. Do not turn this site into a thin product grid.

## Primary clusters

- wholesale rakhi, rakhi wholesale, bulk rakhi
- rakhi supplier, rakhi manufacturer, rakhi exporter (educational, not unverified ownership claims)
- wholesale rakhi India, bulk rakhi India

## Secondary clusters

- buy rakhi wholesale, rakhi wholesale supplier, rakhi bulk supplier
- Rakhi supplier India, Rakhi manufacturer India, Rakhi export from India

## International clusters

Dedicated unique guides (not doorway pages):

- USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, New Zealand, Singapore, Europe

## Informational clusters

- types of rakhi, designer / kundan / beaded / pearl / gemstone / bracelet / kids rakhi
- Rakhi history, Raksha Bandhan, how the festival is observed abroad

## Page-to-keyword mapping

| Intent | URL |

| International hub | `/international-rakhi-wholesale` |
| Quote | `/request-wholesale-quote` |
| Knowledge | `/blog` |

## Internal linking

- Service pages ↔ type pages ↔ country pages ↔ articles ↔ quote form
- Natural anchors. Do not repeat exact-match anchors in every paragraph.

## Content rules

Quality over quantity. No city doorway pages. No keyword stuffing. No invented facts. FAQs in schema match visible FAQs only.

## Technical SEO

- Canonical URLs via `pageMetadata`
- Open Graph + Twitter cards
- JSON-LD: Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, Article, ImageObject
- `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt`
- `/admin` and `/api` noindex / disallowed
- Semantic HTML, one H1 per page, descriptive alt text

## Search Console and analytics

Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GTM_ID` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` when IDs exist. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console after launch.
